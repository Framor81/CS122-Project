POST-IMPRESSIONISM · 1889

The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh

Description

Van Gogh painted this during his stay at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum. The swirling sky depicts a pre-dawn view from his east-facing window. The towering cypress — a symbol of eternity and mourning — anchors the turbulent celestial motion above the sleeping village.
